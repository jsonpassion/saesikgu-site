Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026
Applies to: the iOS app known as Kinwise (also 새식구, 亲戚通, 親戚通, 義実家ノート, Xưng Hô, रिश्ते, เรียกญาติ, Kerabat, Akrabam), version 1.x

ForgeLab, a sole proprietorship run by Jason Lee ("we") respects your privacy and the privacy of the family members you record in the app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored and your choices.

1. The short version

  • Version 1.x has no account and no servers run by us. Everything you enter is stored on your device and in your own iCloud. We cannot see or receive it.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs or tracking.
  • Face detection runs on your device. We never create or store face recognition templates.

2. Information the app handles

2.1 Information you enter (stored only on your device and in your iCloud)

The "Can't eat" field may include health-related details such as allergies. This too stays on your device and in your iCloud and is never sent to us.

2.2 Settings kept only on this device

Quiz history, notification and lock settings, the form-of-address language, and which tips you've seen. These are not synced to iCloud.

2.3 Information we collect

None. In version 1.x we do not collect or store personal information about you or the people you record.

2.4 Information handled by Apple

  • iCloud (CloudKit) stores your data in your iCloud account for syncing and co-editing, under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. The app only checks whether you have an active purchase and never receives payment details.
  • Crash reports may be shared with developers by Apple, anonymously, if you allow this in your device settings.

4. Co-editing (sharing)

Only people you invite can view and edit a shared family. Sharing uses Apple's iCloud sharing, and shared data is stored in the iCloud of the person who created the family. If you stop sharing, the other person can no longer see it. Forms of address, quiz history and notification settings are personal and never shared.

5. Information about other people

The app is for recording information about other people (your partner's family, relatives, friends, coworkers).

  • It stays on your device and in your iCloud and is used only to help you remember.
  • Please record only what you need, and respect what the people concerned would want, especially for photos and health-related details.
  • A person whose information you recorded can ask you to delete it. We do not hold this information and cannot delete it ourselves, but if you contact us we'll explain how.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 14 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Adult users may record a child relative's name, birthday or photo; this stays on the user's device and iCloud. We do not collect children's personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Information stays on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it.
  • People you delete stay in "Recently deleted" for 30 days and are then removed.
  • In Settings, "Erase everything and start over" removes the families you created from this device and your iCloud. Families you were invited to remain in their creator's iCloud and are only removed from this device's list. Deleting the app removes data on this device; iCloud data can be removed in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage.

8. Sharing with third parties, processors and international transfers

We do not sell, share, rent or disclose personal information, use processors, or transfer personal information across borders. The storage location of iCloud data follows Apple's policies.

9. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS Data Protection, and traffic to iCloud is encrypted. You can lock the app with Face ID or your device passcode, and the app hides family information in the app switcher while locked. Face detection stays on the device, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

10. Your rights

You can view, correct, export (Settings > Export family info) and delete your information in the app at any time. The exported backup file (JSON) contains people, photos, notes and relationships as they are. It goes only where you choose (email, the Files app and so on) and is never sent to us. You can restore it as a new family with Settings > Restore from backup file. Because the file contains your family information as is, keep it somewhere safe.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR): because we do not collect or store your personal data, most requests can be completed in the app. You may still contact us about access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.
  • Korea (PIPA): see the Korean version of this policy for the complaint and dispute agencies.
